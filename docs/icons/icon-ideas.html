Faux Film Print: five app icon ideas

Each idea is shown large, at real macOS sizes (128, 64, 32, 16 pt), and together in a Dock below. The little ones tell you most: a good icon still reads at 16.

IDEA 1

The Mini

One tilted Mini card on a deep teal background. It's simple and reads clearly in the Dock: you know straight away it's about instant prints.

IDEA 2

Real + Faux

Two Minis stacked. The one behind is a plain frame, and the one in front is the photo. It hints at mixing your prints in with real ones.

IDEA 3

Cut Lines

An upright card framed by crop marks on charcoal. It's about what the app does (true-size prints you cut out yourself) and looks crisp and technical.

IDEA 4

Fresh Print

A Mini sliding out of a little printer. It's the friendliest of the five and says "this makes prints", though the printer detail gets lost at 16 pt.

IDEA 5

Sheet of Nine

A Letter sheet holding a 3 × 3 grid of Minis, which is exactly what the app prints. It's the most literal of the five, and the grid turns into texture when small.
